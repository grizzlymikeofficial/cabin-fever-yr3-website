# faqs

## house rules

cabin fever is a delicate ecosystem. help us preserve it.

- **respect others** :  zero tolerance for discrimination or harassment in any form. violators will be escorted from the venue. not here, not anywhere
- **respect space** :  zero tolerance for theft or vandalism. our cabin is not your cabin. violators will be escorted at the discretion of the organizers
- **leave no trace** :  all personal belongings must be removed after the party
- **no BYOB alcohol** :  food is OK. we have a private bar + restaurant onsite. this is a state issue — guests do not have a liquor license, but the venue does
- **smoking is allowed** :  only in designated outdoor smoking areas
- **no illegal drugs** :  per idaho state law. this includes marijuana
- **fires allowed** :  in rings and kitchen stoves only
- **mind your footwear** :  prepare for snow/mud outside + a dance floor inside
- **look after yourself and each other** :  please help the event organizers if you see anyone breaking these rules. we understand mistakes happen — organizers will stay aware

## tickets + lodging

all cabin fever tickets include lodging or camping for the weekend. single day tickets are not available for cabin fever year 3. dive in head first, the water is fine...

- **check in** :  friday, 3pm
- **check out** :  monday, 11am
- **shared cabins** :  gets you a space in a 4-6 person cabin or wagon. cabins include a mix of king, queen, and single beds. all cabins have bathrooms and showers. all king/queen beds will be shared between 2-people, let us know who you wanna cuddle with. if you are flying solo, we will try our best to get you a single, however, availability is limited so no promises
- **camping** :  our camping + RV area includes electric and water hookups, picnic tables, and grassy patches to pitch a tent. campers will have access to bathrooms and showers in the bathhouse throughout the weekend. due to limited parking, we will need two campers to arrive in each vehicle. please plan on doubling up or we can help arrange carpool from mike harris.
- **private accom** :  big baller alert. private accom options guarantee you your very own room/cabin on the ranch. purchasing a private room comes with two tickets to cabin fever for you and your boo

## transportation

we recommend planning to drive or carpool to the site. very limited taxi / uber availability

- **driving in** :  venue is a 30-45 minute drive from jackson hole. there is ample parking onsite
- **carpools** :  a rideshare sheet will be sent to ticket holders via email ~1 month before the event to coordinate carpools
- **flying in** :  venue is 45-60 minutes from jackson hole airport (JAC) — 1.5hrs from idaho falls (IDA) — 4hrs from salt lake city (SLC). we recommend you fly in friday and arrange transportation in advance
- **uber / taxi service** :  uber / taxis are inconsistent in rural idaho. we recommend scheduling these 2+ days in advance via local services

## what to pack

spring conditions in wyoming are highly variable… plan for sun, rain, snow, and a ripping hot dance floor

- **dress warmly, wear layers** :  average temps are 30°F - 60°F in may. the dance floor & lodging are separate buildings, you will need to walk between them. coat racks will be available onsite for indoor-outdoor transitions
- **but still pack a swimsuit** :  for the sauna and beach party
- **mind your footwear** :  bring shoes for mud/snow + shoes for dancing
- **bedding and towels provided** :  for all shared and private accom options. campers are on their own.
- **other helpful bits** :  reusable water bottle, headlamp, sunscreen
